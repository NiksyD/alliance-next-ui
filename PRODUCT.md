# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users:

- **Student Attendees**: University students registering for campus events, claiming passes, and checking into venues via QR code.
- **Organization Officers & Event Staff**: Student leaders managing event listings, capacity, and live queue check-in desk scanning.
- **Campus Administrators**: Reviewing attendance statistics, participant verification, and organization compliance.

## Product Purpose

UCEvents is a campus-wide event registration and real-time attendance management system for student organizations. It eliminates friction in college event participation by replacing paper sign-in sheets and scattered Google Forms with seamless digital passes, rapid QR attendance scanning, and instant participation verification.

## Positioning

Unlike generic event platforms (e.g. Eventbrite or Google Forms), UCEvents is purpose-built for university campus life: tied to collegiate student identities, supporting organization-level governance, role-based check-in desks, and verifiable academic/campus attendance records.

## Operating Context

- **Event Discovery & Landing**: Public-facing, high-vibe campus event feed browsed on mobile phones and laptops.
- **Day-of Event Check-in**: High-throughput venue doorways with intermittent network connectivity where officers scan student QR codes on mobile devices in quick succession.
- **Post-Event Analytics**: Desktop dashboards used by student org treasurers and officers to generate attendance reports.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mobile-first responsiveness for students claiming tickets on-the-go.
- Instant QR code pass generation and validation HUD.
- Server-side data security and validation with Zod.
- Full TypeScript strictness and Next.js App Router architecture.

## Brand Commitments

- **Product Name**: UCEvents (Under the Alliance initiative).
- **Core Theme**: "Campus Kinetic" — Vibrant, community-driven, trustworthy, and energizing.
- **Palette Identity**: Institutional UC Blue (`#1875ba`) anchored with UC Gold (`#f5ba13`) achievement accents and Slate neutrals.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved brand tokens, color hierarchy, and design archetype (Option A: Campus Kinetic).
- Next.js 16 + React 19 + Tailwind CSS 4 + TypeScript codebase.

## Product Principles

1. **Frictionless Entry**: Claiming an event ticket or scanning in at the door must take under 3 seconds.
2. **Institutional Credibility with Student Energy**: Look legitimate enough for university administration, while feeling exciting enough for student campus culture.
3. **High-Density Legibility**: Operational screens (check-in counters, attendee rosters) prioritize scan speed and visual confirmation over ornamental decoration.
